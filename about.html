--- layout: null section-type: about title: About --- ## About Me

I am a Principal Research Scientist at the Shanghai Academy of Artificial Intelligence for Science (SAIS). My research centers on gene and RNA foundation models for biological representation learning, and on sequence- and structure-based RNA design.

Representative work includes Nüwa.RNA and ChromFound for foundation modeling, sequence-based siRNA design, and structure-based RNA design through GOLD and SOLD. My related work in cellular and spatial biology includes PAST and FLAG.

Before SAIS, I spent eight years at Ant and Alibaba Group building applied AI systems across computer vision, multimodal perception, and foundation models, with earlier research and engineering experience at Sony and Tractable in Europe.

Toward Programmable and Personalized Medicine. Together with my collaborators, I aspire to turn biological understanding into programmable medicine through nucleic acid therapeutics—using insights into disease mechanisms to guide target selection and RNA design, opening new possibilities to address targets that conventional therapies struggle to reach, and advancing treatments tailored to the biology of individual patients.
