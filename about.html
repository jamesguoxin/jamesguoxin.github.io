--- layout: null section-type: about title: About --- ## About Me

I am a Principal Research Scientist at the Shanghai Academy of Artificial Intelligence for Science (SAIS). Before joining SAIS, I spent eight years at Ant Group and Alibaba Group developing applied AI systems, with earlier research and engineering experience at Sony in Stuttgart and Tractable in London.

My research centers on gene and RNA foundation models for biological representation learning and on sequence- and structure-based RNA design. To connect modeling with scientific reasoning and experimentation, I work on AI scientists and autonomous labs, including contributions to NovaInspire (星河启智), SAIS's open platform for scientific discovery.

After more than a decade in AI, I see today's advances as a turning point for science. AI is accelerating how we learn and connect ideas across disciplines, opening new ways to tackle problems once considered beyond reach.

For example, AI may bring us closer to truly programmable and personalized medicine. Together with my collaborators, I aspire to realize this vision through nucleic acid therapeutics that reach targets conventional therapies struggle to address.
