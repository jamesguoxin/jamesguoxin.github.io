--- layout: null section-type: about title: About --- ## About Me

I am a Principal Research Scientist at the Shanghai Academy of Artificial Intelligence for Science (SAIS). Before joining SAIS, I spent eight years at Ant Group and Alibaba Group working primarily on computer vision and multimodal AI. Earlier, I held research and engineering roles at Sony in Stuttgart and Tractable in London.

My current research centers on building AI models that connect biological understanding with the design of programmable therapeutics. I develop gene and RNA foundation models for biological representation learning and methods for sequence- and structure-based RNA design. Building on this foundation, I explore how AI scientists and autonomous labs can use these models to formulate hypotheses and guide experiments. New experimental data and insights can then feed back to improve the foundation models, closing the loop between modeling and experimentation. This effort includes contributions to NovaInspire (星河启智), SAIS's open platform for scientific discovery.

After more than a decade in AI, I see today's advances as a turning point for science. AI is accelerating how we learn and connect ideas across disciplines, opening new ways to tackle problems once considered beyond reach.

For example, AI may create unprecedented opportunities to bring us closer to truly programmable and personalized medicine. Together with my collaborators, I aspire to realize this vision through nucleic acid therapeutics that reach targets conventional therapies struggle to address.
