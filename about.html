--- layout: null section-type: about title: About --- ## About Me

I am a Principal Research Scientist at the Shanghai Academy of Artificial Intelligence for Science (SAIS). My research focuses on gene and RNA foundation models, spanning single-cell and spatial omics, multimodal biological representation learning, and structure-based RNA design.

Representative work includes PAST for histopathology and spatial transcriptomics, ChromFound for single-cell chromatin accessibility, and FLAG for spatial gene expression prediction, alongside Nüwa.RNA for RNA representations and GOLD and SOLD for structure-based RNA design.

Before SAIS, I spent eight years at Ant and Alibaba Group building applied AI systems across computer vision, multimodal perception, and foundation models, with earlier research and engineering experience at Sony and Tractable in Europe.

Toward Programmable and Personalized Medicine. Together with my collaborators, I aspire to turn biological understanding into programmable medicine through nucleic acid therapeutics—opening new possibilities to address disease targets that conventional therapies struggle to reach, and advancing treatments tailored to the biology of individual patients.
